How registration works

Doing things in the right order.

In the United States, antimicrobial pesticides are regulated by the EPA under the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act (FIFRA). Products must be registered before they can be sold or distributed, and their labels and claims must match what the EPA has approved.

We see that process as a strength. It is why our antimicrobial technology appears on this site only as a technology in development, and why we will not make claims about it until registration is complete.
