Common questions

About our technology in development.

Antimicrobial pesticides must be registered with the U.S. EPA under the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act (FIFRA) before they can be sold or distributed.

Can I purchase your antimicrobial product?

Not at this time. The product is not registered with the U.S. EPA, and it cannot be sold or distributed until registration is granted. We are unable to take orders, provide samples or discuss pricing.

Can you share how the technology works?

Not yet. Until the EPA completes its review, we do not make claims about the technology’s performance, uses or safety. Any future claims will be consistent with the EPA-approved label.

When will it be available?

Timing depends on the EPA’s review and on registration in individual states. We will post updates on this site as the process moves forward.

How can I stay informed?

Email us at info@greenadvanced.com and we’ll follow up when there is news to share.
