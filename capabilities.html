How we make it

Proven at each scale.

Our technology is developed, scaled and manufactured in-house. Our production facility is in China, in a licensed chemical industrial park, and holds environmental impact and safety assessment approvals. [confirm facility]

  • Polymer synthesis developed and run in-house over more than five years
  • Polymer characterized by NMR spectroscopy and gel permeation chromatography, with a university research partner
  • 316L stainless steel reactors: 50 kg laboratory, 500 kg pilot, and 3-ton and 5-ton production
  • Process changes proven at smaller scale before they move up
  • Closed reactors under nitrogen; ammonia captured as ammonium sulfate; exhaust treated before release
  • Each batch tested in the on-site laboratory; management systems being certified
  • U.S. regulatory counsel on EPA registration under FIFRA
